Controls & tips

Curious? Tap a tip to explore.

🧩Add objects

Choose a table and height, then click an object icon. Click it again to select the object already on the table.

🖐️Arrange the tabletop

Drag objects inside the green area in Top View. Start near the robot and leave the approach path and tray clear.

🎯Select a target

Choose Object to pick. The robot approaches from the side and chooses its left or right hand based on the object's position.

▶️Run the attempt

Hit Pick & place and watch the grasp, lift, and tray placement. The hands retract after release. Enjoy!

🔄Reset or edit

Reset restores the robot and your saved layout. Remove deletes the selected object; Clear removes them all.

👀Inspect the motion

Drag the 3D view to orbit and scroll to zoom. The top-right inset is the live view from the onboard D435i camera.

Browser IK: Solves run on your CPU, without cuRobo. Timing, solutions, and motion may differ from cuRobo on CUDA.

Keep the tray clear: A full tray can block a collision-free release path and make the arm hesitate. Remove or Clear placed objects before your next pick.
